# E-commerce-Customer-Sales-Analytics-India
A full end-to-end data analytics project analyzing customer behavior and sales performance on a simulated Indian e-commerce platform across a 24-month period.

**Tools Used:** Excel · SQL (SQLite) · Python (Pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn) · Tableau Public

---

## Project Overview

| Metric | Value |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Total Orders** | 7,600 (raw) → 6,699 (after cleaning) |
| **Total Customers** | 2,400 |
| **Cities Covered** | 28 (across Tier 1, Tier 2, Tier 3) |
| **Product Categories** | 7 |
| **Time Period** | Jan 2024 – Dec 2025 |
| **Total Revenue (Delivered)** | ₹91.03 Lakh |

---

## Key Findings

* **Top Category:** Electronics is the top revenue generator (₹29.6L, 32.6% of revenue) driven by high average order value.
* **Customer Segmentation:** The **Champions** segment (304 customers, 12.7% of customer base) generates 28.5% of total revenue based on RFM modeling.
* **Geographic Insights:** Tier 1 cities (Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru) contribute 76.9% of total revenue.
* **Payment Preference:** UPI dominates payment methods, accounting for 37.4% of all orders.
* **Return Rate Risk:** A 19.85% overall return rate was identified as a key business risk, heavily concentrated in Books, Home & Kitchen, and Electronics.
* **Retention Cohorts:** Cohort analysis shows that most customers churn within 2 months of signup, with only 10–15% retained by month 6.
